Common Questions

        

          How does this calculator estimate monthly living costs?
          
We apply World Bank Household Consumption PPP conversion factors matched to same-year official exchange rates (CC BY 4.0) to a standard remote-worker basket, adjusted for city size and your chosen housing type.

        

        

          Are all 7 data sources genuinely free for commercial websites?
          
Yes. Every source uses CC BY 4.0, CC0 1.0, or Public Domain terms. Paid APIs (Numbeo), Non-Commercial feeds (Ookla Open Data, Cloudflare Radar, Open-Meteo free tier), and copyleft databases (OpenStreetMap ODbL) are intentionally excluded.

        

        

          How do the country map and city numbers stay up to date for free?
          
Natural Earth country borders are pre-projected once into SVG. World Bank economic/governance indicators and GeoNames metadata refresh automatically via a free monthly GitHub Actions schedule with zero API keys.

        

        

          What are the 183-day rule and Europe's 90-day Schengen rule?
          
Spending 183+ days in one country in a year commonly triggers tax residency, while non-EU visitors in Europe's Schengen Area can usually stay up to 90 days in any 180-day period without a special visa.
